A close-knit, flat team

We're a close-knit, flat team with backgrounds in technology, research, banking, law and government. Quite a few different routes to ending up here.

We like thoughtful work, lively disagreements and people who help without making a production of it. Everyone gets to ask questions, contribute ideas and take ownership.

We care a lot. About the product, the people using it and the people building it. We also intend to enjoy ourselves along the way.
